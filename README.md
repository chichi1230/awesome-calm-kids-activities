# Awesome Calm Kids Activities

A small curated list of quiet, creative, and grounding activities for kids.

This list is for parents, teachers, homeschoolers, librarians, and caregivers who
want simple activities for slow afternoons, classroom reset time, rainy days, or
screen-light family routines.

## Mindfulness and Breathing

- [Mindful - Mindfulness for Kids](https://www.mindful.org/mindfulness-for-kids/) - Simple mindfulness ideas for children and families.
- [Positive Psychology - Mindfulness for Kids](https://positivepsychology.com/mindfulness-for-kids/) - Mindfulness activities, worksheets, and classroom-friendly ideas.
- [KidsHealth Mindful Exercises](https://kidshealth.org/en/teens/mindful-exercises.html) - Short mindful exercises written for teens and families.
- [Children's Health - Mindfulness for Kids](https://www.childrens.com/health-wellness/mindfulness-for-kids) - Kid-friendly mindfulness tips from a children's hospital.
- [Coping Skills for Kids](https://copingskillsforkids.com/) - Coping skills ideas and resources for children and caregivers.
- [Action for Happiness - 10 Keys](https://www.actionforhappiness.org/10-keys) - Simple wellbeing ideas that can be adapted into family activities.
- [Healthline Grounding Techniques](https://www.healthline.com/health/grounding-techniques) - Grounding techniques that can be simplified for older kids and teens.

## Quiet Coloring and Art

- [Coloring Pages Story](https://coloringpagestory.com/) - Free printable coloring pages with drawing practice for children.
- [Huebloom Coloring Pages](https://huebloom.art/coloring-pages) - Free printable coloring pages for quiet art time.
- [Crayola Free Coloring Pages](https://www.crayola.com/free-coloring-pages) - Printable coloring pages and activity sheets.
- [Just Color Mandalas](https://www.justcolor.net/relaxation/mandalas/) - Mandala coloring pages for focused coloring sessions.
- [SuperColoring Mandalas](https://www.supercoloring.com/coloring-pages/mandala) - Printable mandala coloring pages in many styles.
- [Red Ted Art](https://www.redtedart.com/) - Paper crafts and simple art projects for kids.
- [DLTK's Crafts for Kids](https://www.dltk-kids.com/) - Crafts, coloring pages, worksheets, and themed printable activities.

## Gentle Movement

- [Kids Yoga Stories - Kids Yoga Poses](https://www.kidsyogastories.com/kids-yoga-poses/) - Simple yoga poses and movement ideas for children.
- [Smiling Mind](https://www.smilingmind.com.au/smiling-mind-app) - Guided mindfulness programs for children, families, and schools.

## Puzzles and Focus Time

- [Math Is Fun Puzzles](https://www.mathsisfun.com/puzzles/) - Logic puzzles, number puzzles, and thinking games.

- [Education.com Worksheets](https://www.education.com/resources/worksheets/) - Printable worksheets by grade, subject, and skill.

## Reading and Learning Breaks

- [Reading Rockets Literacy at Home](https://www.readingrockets.org/literacy-home) - Reading tips, family literacy ideas, and learning resources.

## Notes

- This list is for low-pressure activities, not medical advice.
- Some resources are fully printable, while others are idea pages or guided resources.
- Always check each site's terms before using materials in a classroom, club, or commercial project.
- This list is independent and not affiliated with the sites listed above.

## Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. Please prefer resources that are:
